## Reading

Two statements on one page. The label is the page's licence for the download (dGB's OpendTect project,
with dGB's conversions); CC BY 3.0 AU is what Geoscience Australia releases the original seismic under.
The record carries the label (CC-BY-SA-3.0, `share_alike: true`): it is the statement the page makes
about this download, it is the stricter of the two, and following it also meets CC BY 3.0 AU's
attribution term. `redistribution: allowed` and `attribution: required` hold under either licence, so
neither field rests on the choice; only `share_alike` does. Credit goes to dGB Earth Sciences and to
Geoscience Australia. The stop rule (terms that do not allow redistribution) is not met.

Kinds are those the page states for the project: seismic-3d (full stack in the main download, angle
stacks separately), wellbore-paths (well tracks, deviation surveys), time-depth (the arbitrary
constant-velocity models). The charter table's wellbore-logs and horizons are not stated for the
download; the pack lane reads the archive and the record follows what it holds.

No outline: the page gives one point, a map centre and an area, not the survey's corners. An outline
for this record comes from the archive (the pack lane), not from another record's input.
