Starter projects

A starter project is an open dataset made ready for your tools: one coordinate system, one depth reference, units and null values settled, logs named, delivered in the form OpendTect, ResInsight or Python opens. A recipe writes every file; nobody edits them by hand. Every decision the recipe took is written down below with its source: the publisher, the data itself, or chosen by us and why. Each pack is compared with a reader that is not ours.
